## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-30

- Initial release: `LeanStruct.define` DSL with `attribute` / `attribute?`, `nullable:`, nested structs,
  arrays of nested structs, `class_name:`, `sensitive:`, `LeanStruct::Bool`, immutable instances, recursive `to_h` / `to_hash`.
- Errors for non-Hash input show only the input's class, not its value.
